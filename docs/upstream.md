# Upstream Relationship

QuickRecorder Enhanced is derived from QuickRecorder by lihaoyun6, with 1.6.9
as the initial public codebase reference.

The fork keeps upstream attribution and the AGPL-3.0 license while maintaining
its own product direction, versions, releases, signing identity, update feed,
and documentation.

Useful upstream fixes may be imported after review. Imports should remain
traceable and must not silently remove fork-specific audio, archive,
permission, localization, or validation behavior.

QuickRecorder Enhanced is not endorsed or supported by the upstream author.
